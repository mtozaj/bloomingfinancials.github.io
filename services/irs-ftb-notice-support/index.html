Frequently Asked Questions

I got a notice from the IRS or FTB. What's the first step?

Send us a copy of the notice (both pages, including any explanation codes). We review what the agency is asking for, check the deadline, and confirm the next step. Many notices look alarming but are routine and resolvable.

Will you represent me in front of the IRS?

We provide support that includes reviewing notices, organizing records, reviewing transcripts, and helping draft written responses where appropriate. If your issue requires representation or legal advice beyond our scope, we will let you know and recommend involving the appropriate licensed professional.

My refund was reduced or delayed. Can you help find out why?

Yes. We review the return, the notice, and account transcripts when needed, and explain what's behind the change. If the agency's adjustment is incorrect, we help draft a response or amended return.

Can you set up a payment plan?

We guide you through IRS and FTB payment-plan options based on the balance owed and your situation, and help you submit the request so the account stays in good standing.
