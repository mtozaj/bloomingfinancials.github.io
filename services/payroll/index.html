Payroll

Payroll Services for California Small Businesses

Payroll setup, recurring payroll support, contractor payments, and year-end W-2 and 1099 reporting, coordinated with your bookkeeping and business tax return.

Who This Is For

Small businesses adding their first employee, S-corp owners setting up reasonable compensation, contractor-heavy businesses, and any California employer that wants payroll to connect cleanly with bookkeeping and year-end reporting.

What's Included

  • Payroll setup support
  • Employee payroll support
  • Contractor payment support
  • Payroll-tax filing coordination
  • W-2 and 1099 year-end reporting support
  • Direct deposit setup support
  • Payroll reports for tax filing
  • New-hire and payroll-compliance guidance (where offered)
  • S-corp owner payroll discussion (where offered)

Payroll touches reasonable compensation, W-2s and 1099s, business deductions, and year-end reporting. We coordinate it with your bookkeeping and tax return so nothing falls through the cracks.

How We Work

  1. 1

  2. 2

Pricing Approach

Pricing depends on the type of service, filing complexity, number of income sources, number of states, business activity, bookkeeping condition, payroll needs, and whether prior-year or amended filings are involved. During the consultation, we review your situation and provide clear pricing before work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle payroll for S-corp owners?

Yes. S-corp owners need a reasonable W-2 salary in addition to distributions. We help you set up owner payroll and coordinate it with the S-corp tax return.

Can you help with contractor (1099) payments?

Yes. We support contractor onboarding, recurring payments, and year-end 1099-NEC reporting so contractor relationships stay compliant.

What about new-hire reporting and California payroll-tax filings?

We coordinate payroll-tax filings and provide guidance on new-hire reporting and other California payroll-compliance items so you stay on schedule with federal and state requirements.
