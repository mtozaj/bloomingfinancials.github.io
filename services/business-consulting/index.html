Pricing depends on the type of service, filing complexity, number of income sources, number of states, business activity, bookkeeping condition, payroll needs, and whether prior-year or amended filings are involved. During the consultation, we review your situation and provide clear pricing before work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I form an LLC or elect S corp?

It depends on profit level, owner involvement, and how you'll pay yourself. We model both scenarios (including a reasonable W-2 salary, payroll cost, $800 California minimum tax, and the 1.5% S-corp tax) before recommending a path.

I'm starting a business. Where do I begin?

We help you think through entity choice, bookkeeping setup, payroll versus contractor decisions, and what to expect in your first tax year so you don't get surprised.

Do you provide legal or financial advisory services?

We help business owners understand the tax and bookkeeping side of entity choices and coordinate with legal professionals when formation documents or legal advice are needed. We do not provide investment advice.
