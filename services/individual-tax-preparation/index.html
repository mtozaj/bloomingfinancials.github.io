Frequently Asked Questions

I have RSUs and sold company stock. Can you prepare my return?

Yes. We work with Bay Area tech employees regularly. We confirm RSU income already on your W-2, reconcile broker 1099-B cost basis (including the adjusted basis on supplemental statements), and report short- and long-term gains correctly on Schedule D and Form 8949.

I moved between states this year. Can you handle multi-state returns?

Yes. We prepare federal, California, and other state returns for clients who moved during the year, work remotely across state lines, or hold rental property in another state.

I get my health insurance through Covered California. Can you handle Form 1095-A?

Yes. If you receive Form 1095-A from Covered California, we reconcile the Premium Tax Credit on Form 8962 as part of your return, confirm household income figures, and explain how any advance credit affects your refund or balance due.

Can you file a prior-year or amended return?

Yes. We prepare prior-year federal and California returns and review or prepare amended returns (Form 1040-X and California Schedule X) when income, deductions, or credits need to be corrected.

Do you e-file?

Yes. Blooming Financial is an IRS Authorized E-File Provider. We e-file federal and California individual returns whenever they are eligible for electronic submission.
