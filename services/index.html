Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with clients remotely throughout California?

Yes. We work with clients across California through secure document upload, video calls, and email. Whether you are in Cupertino, San Jose, San Francisco, Los Angeles, San Diego, Sacramento, or anywhere else in the state, you can work with us fully remotely.

Can you handle both individual and business tax returns?

Yes. We prepare individual returns, business returns, and combined situations where an owner needs both their personal and business returns coordinated together.

I am behind on past tax returns. Can you still help?

Yes. We help clients catch up on unfiled federal and California returns, review what records are available, request transcripts when needed, and prepare prior-year and amended returns so accounts can be brought current.

Can you file prior-year tax returns?

Yes. We prepare prior-year federal and California returns, review available records, request IRS or FTB transcripts when documents are missing, and file the returns needed to bring your account current. If amended returns are needed, we review those as well.

My books are not ready for tax season. Can you help?

Yes. We offer catch-up and clean-up bookkeeping so the year is organized before the return is prepared. This is one of the most common reasons clients come to us.

We received an IRS or California FTB notice. What should we do?

Send us a copy of the notice. We review the issue, confirm what the agency is asking for, gather supporting records, and help determine the next steps. Depending on the situation, this may include an amended return, a payment plan, or a written response.

I need to make estimated tax payments. Can you help me figure out how much to pay?

Yes. We calculate quarterly federal and California estimated payments for self-employed individuals, business owners, and clients with significant non-wage income such as RSUs, stock sales, or rental income.
